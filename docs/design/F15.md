# F15 — Visual refresh of the web app

No spec file: the owner's brief (2026-11-20) is the spec. Flag: `FEATURE_F15`. Repo: `discipline` only
(the API already serves any `FEATURE_*=true`; no back-end or analytics change). Base: `main`, after F13 merges.

Goal: one calm, distinctive, consistent look for every page (login, Today, Daily Planner with the F13
views, Analytics, Smart Schedule, Vocabulary), light and dark. Keep React, Tailwind and shadcn/Radix.
Change **design tokens and shared components**, not page-by-page styles. **No behaviour changes.**
With the flag off, today's look is restored exactly.

This document offers **two directions** (§2, §3) with prototypes and screenshots. The owner picks one;
§4–§11 apply to either. Prototypes: `F15-prototype/` (open `index.html` through a local server).
Screenshots: `F15-screens/` — Today and Planner week at 390 and 1440 px, light and dark, plus Uzbek and
Russian at 390 px.

## 1. Stories and acceptance criteria

- **S1 Tokens behind the flag.** AC: with `FEATURE_F15` on, the root element carries `data-look="f15"` and
  the new tokens apply; off, the attribute is absent and every token keeps today's value (snapshot test of
  computed styles on a sample page). The attribute is set before first paint from the last-seen flags, so
  the look never flashes.
- **S2 One self-hosted font.** AC: the chosen font loads from the app's own origin (no Google Fonts request
  with the flag on); it covers Latin, Cyrillic, Uzbek Cyrillic (Ўў Ққ Ғғ Ҳҳ), oʻ/gʻ (U+02BB) and ʼ (U+02BC);
  the OFL licence and source are recorded (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` in `discipline`, and §7).
- **S3 Shared components.** AC: Button, Card, Badge/status chip, Input/Select/Textarea, Dialog/AlertDialog/
  Sheet, Tabs/segmented control, Sidebar, BottomNav, Toast, Skeleton, Progress, OfflineBanner and the F13
  planner pieces (TimeGrid block and marker, weekday pills) read only tokens; no page needs its own colour
  classes. The few hard-coded colours (audit: `SmartSchedule.tsx` `bg-green-600`, `ui/toast.tsx`) move to
  tokens.
- **S4 Statuses never look like failure.** AC: `done` = filled check, `partial` = half ring, `skipped` =
  neutral dashed ring with muted text (no red, no strike-through), `in_progress` = primary ring, rest-kind
  blocks = a calm rest tint. Red (`--destructive`) is used only for destructive actions (delete). Conflict
  and "not this week" markers are neutral and dashed.
- **S5 Accessibility and languages.** AC: every token pair in §5 meets WCAG AA in both themes (a unit test
  computes them from `index.css`); touch targets ≥ 44 px below 768 px (`e2e/touch-targets.ts`); no
  horizontal scroll at 390 px in uz, ru and en on every page; visible focus ring (3:1); `prefers-reduced-motion`
  removes movement.
- **S6 Proof.** AC: Playwright screenshots of every page at 390 and 1440 px, light and dark, flag on, in
  `docs/design/F15-screens/` (the build replaces the prototype shots).

## 2. Direction A — "Morning Paper"

**Mood:** a notebook on a sunny desk. Warm paper, ink and sage; soft, rounded, unhurried. Rest feels like
honey-coloured light, not an empty slot. Friendly without being childish.

- **Font:** Nunito (variable, 200–1000; SIL OFL 1.1; `@fontsource-variable/nunito` 5.3.0). Rounded terminals,
  generous x-height, tabular digits by default (timers and times don't jitter). Body 500, medium 650,
  headings 800, timer 700.
- **Type scale (px):** 13 · 14 · 16 (body) · 15 (buttons) · 18 · 22 · 30 (page title) · 64 timer (56 on a
  phone). Line height 1.55. Headings −0.015 em. Labels in sentence case.
- **Spacing:** 4-px base — 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 44. Cards pad 24 px; page gutter 16 px (phone) / 44 px.
- **Radius:** cards 20 · inner panels 14 · blocks 10 · chips and buttons fully round (pill) · weekday pills 16.
- **Shadows:** soft and warm instead of borders: card `0 1px 2px hsl(30 20% 20% / .05), 0 10px 28px -12px
  hsl(30 25% 20% / .16)`; primary button `0 1px 2px hsl(150 30% 15% / .2)`. Dark: a 1-px border ring plus a
  deep shadow, no glow.
- **Blocks in the grid:** filled with 16 % of their category colour (20 % in dark); the category icon is
  coloured; no stripe. Hour lines only.
- **Icons:** Lucide, stroke 1.75, round caps and joins, 18 px (14 in chips).
- **Motion:** 220 ms, `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`; fades and 4-px rises; the live dot breathes (2.4 s). None of it
  with `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 3. Direction B — "Instrument"

**Mood:** a well-made instrument panel at night. Cool slate, ink and indigo; crisp hairlines, square
corners, numbers you can read at a glance. Calm through order rather than softness.

- **Font:** IBM Plex Sans (variable, 100–700; SIL OFL 1.1, © IBM; `@fontsource-variable/ibm-plex-sans` 5.3.0).
  Engineered, very legible Cyrillic, tabular digits by default. Body 400, medium 500, headings 600, timer 500.
- **Type scale (px):** 12 · 13 · 15 (body) · 14 (buttons) · 16 · 20 · 26 (page title) · 68 timer (56 on a
  phone). Line height 1.5. Labels and chips in small caps style: UPPERCASE, +0.06 em; the brand +0.14 em.
- **Spacing:** 4-px base, tighter — 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32. Cards pad 20 px.
- **Radius:** cards 8 · inner 6 · buttons 6 · chips 4 · blocks 4 · weekday pills 8.
- **Shadows:** none on surfaces; elevation is a 1-px hairline (`--border`) and a surface step. Only popovers and
  dialogs get `0 8px 24px hsl(215 30% 10% / .14)`.
- **Blocks in the grid:** card-coloured with a hairline and a 3-px category stripe on the left; hour lines
  plus faint half-hour lines.
- **Icons:** Lucide, stroke 1.5, square caps, mitred joins, 18 px.
- **Motion:** 140 ms, `cubic-bezier(.3,0,.2,1)`; opacity and position only, no scale; reduced motion: none.

## 4. What both directions share

- **Token set.** shadcn's tokens stay (background, foreground, card, popover, primary, secondary, muted,
  accent, destructive, border, input, ring, sidebar-*) with new values, plus new ones: `--nav-active(-foreground)`,
  `--session-bg`, `--today-col`, `--border-strong`, `--status-{done,partial,skipped}` with `-tint`, `--rest-tint`,
  and `--cat-{focus,study,admin,exercise,rest,personal}` — one per category **kind** (a user's own colour still
  wins on a block's stripe/icon). Non-colour tokens: `--font`, the type scale, `--radius-{card,inner,btn,chip,
  block,pill}`, `--shadow-{card,btn,pop}`, `--icon-stroke`, `--motion-fast`, `--ease`. Tailwind maps them
  (`rounded-card`, `shadow-card`, `bg-status-done-tint`, `font-sans`), so components switch with the attribute.
- **Status language** (S4) and **rest** as a tint, never a gap or grey-out. Category colours are ≥ 3:1 on
  cards (they mark, the text stays `--foreground`).
- **Layout** does not change: the same sidebar, header, bottom nav and page structure; only tokens and shared
  component styles. At 390 px every control is ≥ 44 px.

## 6. Implementation (either direction)

- **Attribute and flag.** `index.html` gets a tiny inline boot script in `<head>` (no CSP is set today) that
  reads the cached flags (`localStorage.features`, already written by `useFeature`) and sets
  `document.documentElement.dataset.look = 'f15'` before React renders; `useFeature('F15')` keeps it in sync
  after `GET /api/features`. next-themes keeps the `.dark` class as today.
- **CSS.** `index.css` keeps today's `:root` and `.dark` blocks untouched and adds `:root[data-look="f15"]` and
  `.dark[data-look="f15"]` (higher specificity), plus the `@font-face` rules. Flag off ⇒ nothing changes.
- **Font.** `@fontsource-variable/<font>` as a dependency (OFL; ~150 KB for the four subsets; only the
  subsets a page uses are fetched, by `unicode-range`), precached by the service worker for offline. With the
  flag on, the Google Fonts request goes: its `@import` moves from `index.css` to a `<link id="legacy-fonts">`
  in `index.html`, which the boot script removes when F15 is on. Fallback metrics via `size-adjust` so the
  swap does not shift layout.
- **Components** (S3): edit `src/components/ui/*` variants to use the new Tailwind keys; `TimeGrid` block/
  marker classes, `BlockCheckIn` chips and `SyncStatus` use the status tokens. No page file changes except
  removing the two hard-coded colours.
- **Monospace.** Times move from JetBrains Mono to the main font's tabular digits (both fonts have them),
  so the app ships one font.

## 8. Screens and states

- **Every page** gets the look through shared components: login, Today (check-in, now card, focus session,
  timeline, shutdown), Daily Planner (Day/Week, form, categories), Analytics, Smart Schedule, Vocabulary,
  NotFound. Empty, loading (skeleton uses `--muted`), error (server message + Retry; not destructive red —
  `--foreground` text with a neutral border) and offline (banner on `--secondary`) states keep their copy.
- **Prototype coverage:** Today and Planner week only (the brief); the build covers the rest.
- **i18n:** no new keys. Long uz/ru strings were checked at 390 px in both directions (no overflow).
- **Observed in the prototypes:** both fonts draw ʻ as a spacing letter, so "qoʻshish" looks a little loose
  (more in Nunito). At 1440 px the week columns are ~150 px, so a conflict marker in its half lane (F13) is
  cramped in either direction (§10 Q2).

## 9. Edge cases

- **Flag flips at runtime:** attribute toggles, no reload; the font loads on demand.
- **First visit, no cached flags:** today's look until `/api/features` answers; then one switch (accepted).
- **Dark mode:** follows next-themes (system or chosen); both palettes defined; the boot script never
  touches `.dark`.
- **Slow network:** `font-display: swap` + metric fallback; offline: precached font.
- **Forced colours / high contrast:** borders and focus rings stay visible (`forced-colors: active` uses
  system colours; dashed skipped outline remains).
- **Reduced motion:** no transitions, no breathing dot.
- **Uzbek Cyrillic users:** covered by the subsets although the UI ships Latin Uzbek.
- **Category colours chosen by users:** stay as chosen; only the six kind defaults change with the look.

## 10. Security, privacy, rollout, open questions

- **Privacy:** no new data. With the flag on, the app stops requesting Google Fonts (a third-party request
  today) — VISION #6.
- **Tests:** unit — the boot script sets/clears the attribute from cached flags; a contrast test reads the
  `data-look` blocks of `index.css` and checks every §5 pair in both themes; computed-style snapshot with the
  flag off equals today's. E2E — every page at 390/1440, light/dark, flag on: screenshots, no sideways
  overflow in en/uz/ru, `expectTouchTargets`, `document.fonts.check` for U+02BB and Cyrillic, no request to
  `fonts.googleapis.com`.
- **Rollout:** web only. Merge `discipline`; set `FEATURE_F15=true` on the API service in staging, look at
  every page, then production. Off again restores today's look at once. After acceptance, a follow-up
  removes the old tokens and the Google Fonts import.
- **Open questions (owner):**
  1. **Which direction, A "Morning Paper" or B "Instrument"?** (Or one with a named change, e.g. B's
     palette with A's radius.)
  2. **Conflict markers at 1440 px** are cramped in a half lane of a ~150 px week column. Keep as is, or add
     a short label ("Didn't fit") with icon-only actions in the week view — that needs two new i18n keys.
  3. **Remove JetBrains Mono** for times (one font, as the brief says) — confirm, since times will look less
     "technical" in A.
