# M5 — Habits, rest & balance

**Goal:** help people keep small habits and stop them from overworking.

## Must
1. **Habits**
   - Name, schedule (daily, chosen weekdays, or X times per week), optional cue ("after X, I will Y"), target (done or a count), optional reminder time.
   - Check off from Today, Planner and Telegram.
2. **Streak rules**
   - "Never miss twice": a streak survives one missed scheduled day and breaks on two consecutive misses.
   - Rest days and freeze days are neutral.
   - One freeze earned per 7 kept days, at most 2 stored, applied automatically and shown to the user.
   - X-per-week habits are judged per week.
3. **Year heatmap** per habit and overall.
4. **Balance view** (weekly): load vs recovery as defined in VISION, per day.
5. **Gentle warnings** — each shows why, one suggested action, and "don't show this kind again":
   - Planned focus exceeds the user's max per day (shown while planning).
   - No rest day in the last 7 days.
   - Average sleep below the user's target for 3+ days (only with ≥3 check-ins in that period).
   - A block within 60 min of bedtime.
   - 2 h of focus without a break (from sessions).
   - No medical wording; tips stay general.
6. **Rest-day mode:** user marks a day (or uses the weekly rest day). No focus targets, task reminders silenced, streaks neutral; Today shows rest suggestions.

## Acceptance criteria
- Streak edge cases covered by table-driven tests: one miss, two misses, miss + rest day + miss, freeze use, week boundary, timezone change.
- Each warning fires on its fixture and stays silent on the opposite fixture.
- Rest day: zero task notifications sent (worker test).

## Events
`habit_created`, `habit_checked`, `streak_freeze_used`, `warning_shown`, `warning_dismissed`, `rest_day_set`.
